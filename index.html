1. Information We Process

To compute your estimated body composition and track progress toward your goals, the application processes user-supplied fitness metrics entered within the app:

Body Measurements

Gender, age, height, body weight, neck circumference, waist circumference, and hip circumference (for females).

Calculated Results

Body Fat % (U.S. Navy Method & BMI Method), Body Mass Index (BMI), Fat Mass, Lean Body Mass, and ACE category classifications.

Target Goals

Your target body fat percentage and target weight goals configured within the Goal & Progress tab.

Application Preferences

Measurement unit system (Metric: cm/kg or US Customary: in/lbs), reminder scheduling preferences, and theme preferences.

No account or login is required: You are not asked for your name, email address, phone number, location, or credentials.

2. How Your Data Is Used

All data processed by the application is utilized solely to perform the on-device functions requested by you:

  • Calculations: Applying scientific formulas (U.S. Navy Body Fat equations, Jackson & Pollock benchmarks, and BMI algorithms) locally on your device.
  • Trend Analytics: Plotting historical change charts, days in progress, and progress bars towards your custom targets.
  • Local Reminders: Triggering periodic local notifications at your chosen time to remind you to log your measurements.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Your information is persisted strictly in your device's local application sandbox storage (via encrypted/isolated AsyncStorage).

  • No Cloud Uploads: No measurements or logs are ever transmitted to any external server or cloud database.
  • Data Retention: Data remains stored locally until you choose to delete individual history entries, use the in-app "Reset All Data" option in Settings, or uninstall the application.
  • Device Backups: If you use standard operating system backups (such as Google Drive Device Backup or Apple iCloud Device Backup), your local app sandbox data may be included in your encrypted device backup under your personal OS account.

4. Device Permissions

The application requests only minimal, necessary system permissions to support user-selected features:

Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Enables the app to display local reminder notifications when scheduled by the user in Settings.

Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)

Allows Android to accurately trigger your chosen reminder time even while the device is in low-power or idle mode.

These permissions operate entirely offline on your phone. Notifications contain no remote tracking beacons or third-party ad payloads. You can revoke notification permissions at any time via your device settings.

5. Social & Image Sharing

The app features optional image sharing (generating sleek result cards and progress cards to share with fitness coaches, friends, or social media):

  • On-Device Generation: Share cards are rendered dynamically on your device canvas.
  • Native Share Sheet: When you tap "Share Image Card" or "Copy Text Caption", the app hands the image or text to your device's native operating system share dialog.
  • You Control Sharing: Nothing is shared automatically without your explicit action, and no copies are sent to or stored on our servers.

6. Third-Party Services & Advertising

No Third-Party Trackers or Analytics: The application does not embed third-party analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Meta Pixel).

No Advertisements: We do not integrate advertising networks or data broker SDKs. We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Because our application does not collect personal identification, online contact info, or upload data to remote servers, we do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, please contact us.

8. Your Rights & Data Deletion (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), users have rights regarding their data. Because your data is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Access: All data is immediately visible and accessible to you inside the Calculator, History, and Progress screens.
  • Correction: You can update your profile parameters or edit/re-calculate any entry at any time.
  • Permanent Deletion: You have autonomous, instantaneous deletion control:
    • Delete individual log entries directly in the History tab.
    • Use the Reset All Data button in Settings to permanently wipe all stored history, profile values, and preferences.
    • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all local data from your device storage.
